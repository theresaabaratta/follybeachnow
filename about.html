Who We Are

We're a small, independent team that puts this site together because we enjoy sharing what's going on around town. Our goal is simple: keep things easy to find, up to date and friendly for anyone who stops by.

Everything here is written by people who spend real time in the area. We keep an eye on what's new, update listings when things change and try to answer the questions we get asked most.

This site is not affiliated with any government office or official organization. Details like hours, dates and prices can change, so it's always a good idea to confirm directly with the business or organizer before you go.

Independent

We're locally run and not tied to any single business or organization.

Kept Current

We update pages as things change and welcome corrections from readers.

Open to Ideas
