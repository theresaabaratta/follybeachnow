Eat & Drink on the Island

Where to Eat on Folly Beach

Folly Beach dining is all about fresh Lowcountry seafood, flip-flop friendly patios and a good drink with an ocean breeze. Whether you want a sit-down dinner, a quick taco between beach sessions or a slow brunch, here's what the island does best.

Seafood Casual Eats Brunch & Coffee Bars & Drinks Sweet Treats

Seafood on Folly Beach

Lowcountry Classic

Local Shrimp & Grits

The dish to order at least once. Look for local shrimp over creamy stone-ground grits.

Hands-On

Steamed Crab & Shrimp

Casual crab shacks serve buckets of steamed seafood best eaten on an outdoor deck.

Waterfront

Oceanfront Dinner

Book a table with a view for sunset and fresh catch of the day.

Casual Beach Eats

Beach Staple

Fish & Shrimp Tacos

A Folly favorite — quick, fresh and easy to grab between beach sessions.

Crowd Pleaser

Burgers & Baskets

Laid-back grills serving burgers, wings and fried baskets on shaded patios.

Beach Day

Pizza & To-Go

Grab pizza, subs or sandwiches to take back to the rental or the sand.

Brunch & Coffee

Morning Ritual

Beach Breakfast

Biscuits, omelets and breakfast plates to fuel a morning surf or walk on the beach.

Brunch & Mimosas

Slow weekend brunches with Bloody Marys, mimosas and Southern favorites.

Grab & Go

Coffee & Smoothies

Iced coffee, smoothies and acai bowls for a quick start before hitting the sand.

Dessert Stops

Folly Dining Tips

Summer weekends get busy — eat early or late to skip the wait.

Call ahead for larger groups; many spots don't take reservations.

Plan dinner around sunset if you want a view.

Hours change seasonally, so check before you head out.
