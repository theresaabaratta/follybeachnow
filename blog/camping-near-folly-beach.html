Camping Near Folly Beach

Where to pitch a tent or park the RV within an easy drive of the sand, plus how to make the daily beach run painless.

Camping is prohibited everywhere inside the City of Folly Beach, sand included, and the county-run park and pier lots ban overnight parking. The good news: several solid campgrounds sit on James Island and Johns Island, close enough that your morning coffee and your first swim at Folly Beach can happen within the same half hour.

James Island County Park Is the Closest Full-Service Base

The Campground at James Island County Park sits inside a 643-acre county park at 871 Riverland Drive, roughly five miles from the island. It covers nearly every camping style — RV sites with full hookups, tent pads, primitive and group sites, plus cottages for anyone who would rather sleep under a roof. Trails, a dog park, bike rentals and a seasonal water park keep downtime easy, and golf carts are allowed on park roads during daylight hours.

Book through Charleston County Parks or by phone at (843) 795-4386; summer weekends fill early. Tall rigs should come in via Folly Road to Camp Road to Riverland Drive, since low branches on other stretches of Riverland can scrape. In November and December the park hosts its holiday light show, which can slow traffic inside the gates, so build in extra time.

Hanscombe Point Brings Farm Camping Minutes Away

For something quieter, Hanscombe Point Plantation is a 300-acre working farm that has been in the Legare family for nine generations. It sits about three and a half miles from Folly, offers a handful of tent and RV sites and is booked through Hipcamp. Expect open fields, farm animals and a far slower pace than a big campground.

Oak Plantation Campground Handles Big Rigs

Oak Plantation Campground at 3540 Savannah Highway on Johns Island has more than 200 sites under live oaks, a pool and year-round availability, and it has been family run for over four decades. It is the easiest pick for large motorhomes. The trade-off is Highway 17 traffic, which backs up near the Main Road light at rush hour, so plan beach runs early or late in the day.

Edisto and Mount Pleasant Round Out the Options

If waking up beside the ocean matters more than proximity, Edisto Beach State Park runs two campgrounds — one on the oceanfront, one under live oaks by the marsh — plus cabins, about an hour's drive south. Across the harbor, the Mount Pleasant / Charleston KOA Holiday sits on a historic plantation and works well if you plan to split time between Folly and Shem Creek or Isle of Palms.

Making the Daily Beach Run Work

Because you will be driving onto the island each day, parking is the main thing to plan. Folly Beach County Park at 1100 West Ashley Avenue has a paid lot with restrooms and is the easiest family setup. Street parking is allowed in many spots but must follow the city parking rules — all four tires off the pavement and no blocking driveways.

Leave the cooler of beer and anything glass at camp: alcohol and glass are not allowed on the beach. Dogs are off the sand from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. between May 1 and September 30, must be leashed otherwise and are never allowed on the pier. Grab breakfast or lunch on Center Street from our dining picks, check the live surf cams before you pack the boards, and see what is on the events calendar for the week you are in town.

Planning a longer stay? Our vacation planner covers rentals, Center Street and island rules, and the Folly Beach Now homepage has everything else happening on the island.

At a Glance

  • On-island camping: not allowed
  • Closest campground: James Island County Park, ~5 miles
  • Farm stay: Hanscombe Point, ~3.5 miles
  • Big rigs: Oak Plantation, Johns Island
  • Beach parking: County Park lot or legal street spots
