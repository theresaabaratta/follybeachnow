Folly Beach Girls Weekend

Slow mornings, sunset cruises, salty hair and long dinners with your best friends. Here's how to plan a laid-back girls trip to Folly Beach.

Friends, Sun & Salt Air

A Girls Trip to Folly Beach, Done Right

Whether it's a birthday, a college reunion or just a long-overdue catch-up, Folly Beach is an easy yes for a girls getaway. The island is small and walkable, the vibe is relaxed and the ocean does most of the work. You can spend the whole trip barefoot, then drive 20 minutes into Charleston if the group wants a day of shopping downtown.

This guide covers the whole girls weekend in order: easygoing activities, where to eat, brunch with the crew and where to stay together.

Things To Do

Girls Weekend Activities on Folly Beach

Keep the schedule light: one thing on the water, one thing on the sand and plenty of time for nothing at all. These are the girls trip activities worth planning around.

Sunset Cruise

Sunset Cruise With Folly Tiki

Folly Tiki runs public sunset cruises on its tiki catamaran as well as private charters. It's a low-effort way to see dolphins and watch the sky turn pink with your friends.

Public cruises or private charters
Visit Website
On the Water

Paddleboard the Folly River Marsh

A guided stand-up paddleboard or kayak tour through the calm creeks behind the island. You'll get marsh views, birds, maybe a dolphin, and a workout that doesn't feel like one.

Beginner-friendly, morning is calmest
Book a Paddle Tour
Try Something New

Group Surf Lesson

Folly is the East Coast's laid-back surf town, and the gentle beach breaks are great for first-timers. Book a group lesson and cheer each other on when someone finally stands up.

Lessons available on the island
Book a Lesson
Shopping

Shop Center Street

Browse beachy boutiques, surf shops like the long-running McKevlin's and souvenir stores along Center Street. Grab an iced coffee and take your time.

Center Street, Folly Beach
McKevlin's Surf Shop
Beach Day

Quiet Beach Day at the County Park

Folly Beach County Park on the west end of the island is quieter than the pier area, and it has restrooms and showers. Pack books, snacks and a big blanket for a real reset day.

West end of Folly Beach
Park Info
Entertainer

Michael At Your Place

Dont leave your airbnb a interactive energetic show is coming to you, just call or text to book.

Folly Beach Pier
Book a Show
Leave room to do nothing. The best part of a Folly Beach girls weekend is usually the unplanned afternoon on the porch, so don't overbook it.

Mix one "dress up a little" dinner with easy, sandy-feet meals. These are our picks for eating well with friends on the island.

Home Base

Where to Stay for a Folly Beach Girls Getaway

Smaller groups have more options. A cozy cottage feels like a sleepover, a marsh-view house is perfect for sunset porch nights, and the oceanfront hotel keeps things easy.

Cozy Cottage

Classic Folly Beach Cottage

A 3–4 bedroom beach cottage a few blocks from the sand is the sweet spot for most girls trips. Look for a screened porch and an outdoor shower.

Best for 4–8 friends

Sunset Views

Marsh-View Home on the River Side

Rentals facing the Folly River trade ocean views for some of the prettiest sunsets on the island. They're often quieter and better value, too.

Folly River side of the island

Easy Mode

Tides Folly Beach Hotel

Book a few oceanfront rooms side by side and let someone else make the beds. You get a beachfront pool, a tiki bar and the pier right outside.

1 Center St, Folly Beach
