Something Happening Every Season

What's Going On at Folly Beach

From spring festivals on Center Street to golf-cart parades in December, Folly Beach has a packed community calendar. Here's a season-by-season look at the island's favorite events, plus the live music and weekly happenings that keep things lively year-round.

Spring Summer Fall Winter Live Music & Weekly
Spring

March – May

Spring Events on Folly Beach

April

Sea & Sand Festival

A springtime island tradition with sand sculpting, local vendors, food and family activities.

Spring

Beach Sweeps & Cleanups

Volunteer cleanups that help keep the beach, dunes and marsh in great shape ahead of summer.

Spring

5K Runs & Charity Races

Run or walk the island streets and beachfront in community races that support local causes.

Summer

June – August

Summer Events on Folly Beach

July

4th of July on Folly

The busiest beach day of the year, with holiday celebrations around the island. Come early and plan your parking.

Summer

Surf Contests

Local surf competitions bring out riders of all ages. Great for watching from the pier or the sand.

Summer

Fishing Tournaments

Anglers compete from boats and the pier during peak summer fishing season.

Fall

September – November

Fall Events on Folly Beach

October

Halloween Carnival

Costumes, games and treats for the kids — a local favorite every October.

Fall

Oyster Season Kickoff

Cooler weather means local oysters are back on menus and at roasts around the area.

Fall

Quieter Beach Days

Warm water, smaller crowds and easier parking make fall one of the best times to visit.

Winter

December – February

Winter Events on Folly Beach

December

Christmas Parade

Golf carts, floats and holiday lights rolling down Center Street, Folly style.

January 1

New Year's Day Plunge

Start the year with a brave (and chilly) dip in the Atlantic alongside a crowd of locals.

Winter

Off-Season Sunsets & Shelling

Empty beaches, great shelling and some of the year's best sunsets over the marsh.

Year-Round

Every Week

Live Music & Weekly Happenings

Weekends

Live Music on Center Street

Bars and restaurants around the island host local bands, acoustic sets and DJs, especially Thursday through Sunday.

Weekly

Trivia & Open Mic Nights

Plenty of spots run weekly trivia, karaoke and open mic nights through the year.

Daily

Sunrise at the Pier

Not an event, exactly, but a Folly ritual worth setting an alarm for.

Event Day Tips

Arrive early — parking fills up fast on big event days.

Follow posted beach and parking rules; they're enforced.

Dates change year to year, so confirm with the organizer before you go.

Leave only footprints — pack out what you bring.
