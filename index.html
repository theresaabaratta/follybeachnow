Welcome to Folly

The Edge of America

Folly Beach is Charleston's funky, easygoing beach town: a six-mile barrier island with a surf-town soul, colorful beach houses and a Center Street full of local shops, bars and restaurants. Whether you're here for a day trip, a week-long rental or you're lucky enough to live here, Folly Beach Now keeps you in the loop.

  • Catch a sunrise from the Folly Beach Pier
  • Surf or watch the pros at the Washout
  • Walk out to see the Morris Island Lighthouse
  • Kayak or paddleboard the Folly River marsh
  • Shop and grab a bite along Center Street
